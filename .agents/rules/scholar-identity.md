# Official Scholar Identity Rule

The official name of the scholar must ALWAYS and EXCLUSIVELY be written as:
**«حجت الاسلام دکتر زنجیر زن حسینی»**

Do NOT truncate to "دکتر زنجیرزن", "استاد حسینی", or any other partial form in user-facing texts, page titles, footer, headers, swagger docs, or metadata.
